# EnVi Dictionary — Privacy Policy

EnVi Dictionary does not collect, store, or sell any personal data.

- When you look up a word, only that word is sent to Google Translate (`translate.googleapis.com`) to get its Vietnamese meaning and pronunciation audio. No page content, URL, or identifier is sent.
- Your settings (trigger mode) are stored locally in your browser via `chrome.storage`.
- No analytics, no tracking, no accounts.
